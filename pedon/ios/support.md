# PEDON for iPhone — Support

PEDON for iPhone is the on-site companion to the free, open-source PEDON desktop editor.
You need a Mac running the editor, a measured textured mesh scan of your site, and a design
exported with **··· → See it on site**. See the [setup guide](README.md) for installation,
connection, alignment and planting-guide instructions.

## Connecting

Keep the Mac viewer running, open its phone connection in **See it on site**, and connect
the iPhone to the same Wi-Fi. Enter the address from that sheet in the app's Settings.
Allow camera and local-network access when prompted. If a permission was denied, change it
in **iPhone Settings → Apps → PEDON**. A VPN or guest Wi-Fi that separates devices can prevent
the local connection.

## Loading a changed design

Prepare the new design in the Mac's **See it on site** sheet, then tap **↻** on the phone.
Reopening normally uses the saved offline copy. Alignment can carry over when the scan and
export reference frame are unchanged.

## Alignment and planting

Choose fixed, recognizable features on the original scan, then match those same features
with the camera. Use **Show whole scan** to recover the picker view. After alignment, wait for
**Position saved on this iPhone** before leaving. On reopening, point the camera around the
same area. If recognition fails, **Align again** lets you rematch the saved scan points.
AR tracking can drift: check another fixed reference and measured distances before digging.

**Planting guide** shows plant centers. Select a target for a translucent mature plant preview
and available details. **Individual plants** controls visibility; **Hide controls** clears the
view. Saved sizes describe the design, not a guarantee of how a plant will grow.

## Contact the maintainers

[Open a support request or report a bug](https://github.com/FelisAI/pedon/issues/new) in the
public PEDON issue tracker. Include the app version shown in Settings, your iPhone model,
iOS version, and the steps that led to the problem. Do not post personal scans, site addresses,
camera pictures or credentials. A GitHub account is needed to post; existing reports can be
read without signing in.
